## How to play the current build

`bash _build/audit-20261003/envoy-look/play.sh` opens a Classic run under the rule host from a frozen build
(`frozen-f`) with the mod copied from the tree. `envoy classic <fighter>` picks a fighter; `envoy grant <keystone>`
gives a keystone at once. Local-only drive models come from a mod that is never committed.
