# Boss fights and an after-Master-Hand phase

**2026-09-27. Research only; no game run.** Source inspected in the separate `melee/` `pc-port` checkout, plus the current public Lua and Geno docs. This note narrows the [adventure capability survey](adventure-building-blocks.md) to the Hands, Giga Bowser, Classic's final match and possible continuation hooks. It supersedes that survey's brief boss paragraph where this note is more specific. Function names inherited from the decomp often remain address based. **S/M/L** below mean a small integration, a contained native feature, or a larger engine/content/networking project; they are scope estimates, not test results.

## 1. What the bosses are

| Boss | Implementation and fight rules |
|---|---|
| Master Hand | `CKind_MasterH` maps to `Ft_Kind_MasterH` (internal kind `0x1B`). It is a real fighter object with a dedicated motion-state table, `PlMh.dat`/`PlMhAJ.dat`, lasers and bullets. Its load hook sets `no_normal_motion` and `no_kb`, places it from boss attributes and starts its special wait/entry logic. The wait animation picks attacks from weighted, random state tables and coordinates with Crazy Hand's motion state; it is not a generic CPU fighter AI ([kinds](../melee/src/melee/ft/forward.h#L129), [character mapping](../melee/src/melee/pl/player.c#L69), [load](../melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftMasterHand/ftmasterhand.c#L610), [AI/state choice](../melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftMasterHand/ftmasterhandwait10.c#L200)). |
| Crazy Hand | `CKind_CrezyH` maps to `Ft_Kind_CrezyH` (`0x1C`). It likewise has its own fighter state table, `PlCh.dat`/`PlChAJ.dat`, laser/bullet/bomb items, `no_normal_motion` and `no_kb`. Its wait state chooses attacks based partly on Master Hand's state, and the paired attacks use `ftBossLib_GetFighterGObj` to find the other Hand ([kinds](../melee/src/melee/ft/forward.h#L130), [load](../melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCrazyHand/ftcrazyhand.c#L604), [AI choice](../melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCrazyHand/ftcrazyhandwait10.c#L174), [lookup](../melee/src/melee/ft/ftbosslib.c#L219)). |
| Giga Bowser | `CKind_GKoops` maps to `Ft_Kind_GKoops` (`0x1F`). It is a separate fighter/data set (`PlGk.dat`) whose special motion table calls Bowser/Koopa special-move callbacks. `ftGk_Init_OnLoad` reuses Bowser initialization and registers Bowser flame; its death callback restores Bowser-like attributes. Adventure schedules a Bowser fight, transformation visual scene, Giga fight and defeated visual scene as separate major-mode states. The 1P match builder's Hand HP branch does **not** include Giga Bowser: its Adventure encounter uses ordinary fighter stock/KO rules and special scale/armor behavior rather than the Hands' stamina-zero finish ([kinds](../melee/src/melee/ft/forward.h#L133), [Giga motion/data](../melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftGigaKoopa/ftgkoopa.c#L22), [Giga load/death](../melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftGigaKoopa/ftgkoopa.c#L291), [Adventure finale states](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmadventure.c#L105), [encounter rows](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmadventure.c#L1208), [1P setup](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L818)). |

**HP is stamina-style damage accounting.** The 1P builder initially gives either Hand `hp = 0x12C` (300), sets the special stamina/HUD flag, one stock, boss spawn direction and team; Crazy Hand begins as an inactive slot. `gmvs.c` copies the configured `hp` to `Player_SetOtherStamina`. At match start, however, `fn_8017C71C` obtains a difficulty-based HP value from Master Hand's attributes and **replaces Master Hand's maximum** with it; Crazy Hand's later spawn uses that same cached value. Thus 300 is the setup value, not necessarily the active Classic boss HP. `Player_GetRemainingHP` computes `max_stamina - accumulated_damage`, clamped at zero, and the HUD shows remaining HP when that special flag is set ([boss setup](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L818), [match-start override](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L259), [Crazy spawn](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L68), [attribute lookup](../melee/src/melee/ft/ftbosslib.c#L261), [stamina copy](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmvs.c#L1680), [remaining HP](../melee/src/melee/pl/player.c#L1104), [HUD](../melee/src/melee/if/ifstatus.c#L564)).

**Final Destination is both arena and boss presentation.** Classic's fixed boss matchup is stage ID `0xB0`, with `CKind_CrezyH`, `CKind_MasterH`, then an empty kind. The stage map resolves that external ID to `Gr_Kind_Last` (Final Destination); the regular internal `St_Kind_Last` is `0x20`. `grLast_8021B2E8` explicitly recognizes `0xB0` and event `0xFB`, advancing FD background phases at thresholds of average Hand HP (`gm_8016ECE8`). The `0xB0` audio entry is also distinct. Reusing the *geometry* on another encounter ID need not reproduce the Classic boss backdrop/music cues ([boss matchup](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmclassic.c#L409), [matchup assignment](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmclassic.c#L686), [stage kinds](../melee/src/melee/gr/forward.h#L175), [stage map](../melee/src/melee/gr/stage.c#L450), [FD progression](../melee/src/melee/gr/grlast.c#L593), [HP average](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmvs.c#L2436), [audio entry](../melee/src/melee/lb/lbaudio_ax.c#L476)).

## 2. Classic's boss fight script and exit

1. **Mode and arena setup.** `gm_Mode_Classic_States` has alternating intro and `GS_VS` states; final intro `0x50` and fight `0x51` use `gmClassic_801B3500`, `gmClassic_801B3A34`, `gmClassic_801B3B40`. Classic's `0x20` matchup flag selects the fixed `0xB0` row. The intro preloads the protagonist, both Hand character entries, stage and audio; the fight setup calls `gm_8017CE34` with those characters, time and stage ([state table](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmclassic.c#L347), [matchup data](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmclassic.c#L409), [matchup selection](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmclassic.c#L686), [intro preload](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmclassic.c#L775), [fight builder call](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmclassic.c#L973)).
2. **Slots and start callback.** The builder puts Crazy Hand in reserved slot 1 (slot type 3/inactive) and Master Hand in slot 2 with boss HP flags; `boss_count` sets boss-specific match rules and `on_match_start = fn_8017C71C`. That callback creates a persistent GObj process `fn_8017C1A4`, caches difficulty-based Crazy HP, and initializes Master/Crazy boss state. These slot numbers are **zero-based C player slots**; public `gd.player` ports are one-based ([builder](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L769), [boss rules](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L878), [controller start](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L259), [Lua numbering](../docs/scripting.md#L151)).
3. **Master Hand entry and Crazy Hand threshold.** Controller state 0 suppresses HUD and waits for Master Hand's entry motion to finish; state 2 restores HUD and standard camera; state 3 is the active solo-Hand fight. At `Player_GetRemainingHP(2) <= half of cached HP`, `fn_8017EDDC` admits Crazy Hand only in Classic at difficulty `>= 2` (Hard/Very Hard index), with no continue and accumulated time `< 0x5208` frames. Controller state 1 freezes/cinematically stages the entry and calls `fn_8017C0C8` to spawn Crazy Hand into slot 1; state 4 then watches both HP values. If Master dies before the threshold branch, state 3 goes straight to final state 7 ([controller](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L91), [threshold](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L159), [eligibility](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17EB.c#L146), [spawn](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L68)).
4. **HP-zero death, then delayed finish.** Damage updates fighter percent and player stamina bookkeeping in `Fighter_TakeDamage_8006CC7C`; `ftCo_800C8C84` checks exact remaining HP zero and `fn_800C8E74` sends Master to `ftMh_MS_343_80151484` or Crazy to `ftCh_GrabUnk1_8015ADD0`. Those start Hand-specific damage/death motion and SFX. When their animation timer expires, `ftCo_800D4F24(..., 0)` runs death cleanup and `ftCo_800BFD04` puts the fighter into invisible `Sleep` with an inactive camera box. Thus **HP reaching zero, entering death motion, and visual disappearance are distinct points**; the Hand is not necessarily immediately removed from the fighter list ([damage bookkeeping](../melee/src/melee/ft/fighter.c#L3214), [zero check/dispatch](../melee/src/melee/ft/ft_0C8C.c#L25), [Master death motion](../melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftMasterHand/ftmasterhanddamage0.c#L54), [Crazy death motion](../melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCrazyHand/ftcrazyhandtaggrab.c#L46), [cleanup](../melee/src/melee/ft/ft_0D4D.c#L84), [Sleep](../melee/src/melee/ft/ftcolanim.c#L47)).
5. **Final flash and match end.** With both Hands at zero HP (or just Master in the solo path), controller state 7 hides HUD, focuses the camera, disables the match timer, triggers boss effect/SFX, slow motion, screen flash and repeated quakes. Only after its attribute-driven delay does it call `gm_8016B33C(8)` and `gm_8016B328()`; the latter sets `terminate_match`, which `gm_GetMatchOutcome` converts to `OUTCOME_TERMINATED`. There is no single `on_explode` call here. The perceived explosion is Hand animation plus this controller's effects. If one of two Hands dies, state 6 plays a shorter camera/slow-motion beat and returns to state 5 to fight the survivor ([controller states 3–7](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L159), [match termination setters](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmvs.c#L286), [outcome](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmvs.c#L888)).
6. **Results and next scenes.** The `GS_VS` scene packages `MatchEnd`, outcome, timer and stage result into `MatchExitInfo`; Classic's `gmClassic_801B3B40` records the clear/failed row and calls `gm_8017D7AC` for stock, time, score and game-over routing. After a successful final row it calls `gm_8017CBAC(..., 0x15)`, saving completion/records and making `GM_CLASSIC_GOVER` pending. That mode's states are `GS_REGEND_TOYFALL` → `GS_STAFFROLL` → `GS_MOVIE_END` → `GS_REGEND_CONGRATS`, followed by menu/challenger routing. The minor-scene handlers are in `gmscdata.c`. **Classic does not route this clear through the ordinary `GS_RESULTS` minor scene.** The `0x68`/`GS_COMING_SOON` state in the Classic table is a fallback table entry, not the normal successful-boss next scene, because the clear callback changes mode first ([VS exit packaging](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmvs.c#L2345), [Classic exit](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmclassic.c#L1012), [1P result accounting](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L893), [clear/save/mode switch](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L424), [mode table](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmscdata.c#L566), [clear scene table](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmgover.c#L17), [minor-scene handlers](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmscdata.c#L169), [routing priority](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_1A3F.c#L158)).

## 3. Continuation hooks and required surface

| Hook or route | What it can do today | Gap and size for the first usable slice |
|---|---|---|
| Observe HP/death from Lua | Public `on_frame()` sees `gd.scene()`, `gd.match()` and `gd.players()`; a Hand can be identified by fighter kind, damage (`percent`) and action. The later LAB additions include queued `on_action_change` and `on_hit`, and Geno profiles have fighter-local `on_frame`/`on_action`/`on_hit`. These can signal or log a transition offline. Public player state exposes damage but **not** `Player_GetRemainingHP` or max stamina, and neither surface has an `on_boss_death`/`on_boss_defeated` event. An action-change event alone is not proof of a completed defeat ([public hooks/state](../docs/scripting.md#L130), [player fields](../docs/scripting.md#L175), [LAB events](../melee/docs/geno.md#L395), [Geno dispatch](../melee/docs/geno.md#L1959)). | **S** to expose an explicit, read-only boss-HP/death event or inspect it in native code; **M** if it must become a reusable event contract with pre/post death stages and rollback semantics. The LAB event queue runs after engine sites, before `on_frame`; it cannot cancel an already-entered native finish. |
| Hold this fight and start another phase | The native `fn_8017C1A4` controller already owns the HP gates, cinematic beats, HUD and eventual termination. The decisive branch is state 3's HP-zero transition to state 7, with a second gate in state 4/5 when both Hands reach zero. A custom mode can substitute its own phase state and call the original-style finish only after the follow-up. `gm_8016B328` is the actual match-end request ([controller](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L159), [termination](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmvs.c#L286)). | **M**: native mode/controller state and a defined once-only trigger. Lua `gd.pause()` freezes logic (so no fight proceeds), and no Lua call cancels the 1P controller or keeps the current scene alive by itself ([pause behavior](../docs/scripting.md#L146), [gameplay API](../docs/scripting.md#L286)). |
| Spawn a fighter/boss mid-match | Vanilla already spawns Crazy Hand via `fn_8017C0C8` → `gm_8016EDDC` into a pre-reserved slot, with `PlayerInitData`, HP, team and flags. `gm_8016EDDC` checks slot availability and a match state flag; the original intro preloads both Hand assets. Geno can overlay an existing fighter's scripts/actions and spawn fighter-owned *articles*, but those articles are not a new fighter ([Crazy spawn](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L68), [spawn preconditions](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmvs.c#L2458), [preload](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmclassic.c#L880), [Geno scope](../melee/docs/geno.md#L7), [articles](../melee/docs/geno.md#L1872)). | **S/M** for the already-reserved Crazy Hand in a contained custom mode, depending on camera/cleanup validation; **M** for a reusable spawn/preload/slot/HP API for an existing fighter; **L** for arbitrary new fighter content or bosses. Public `gd.*` has no `spawn_fighter`, boss HP setter, or live roster/rule rewrite ([Lua gameplay API](../docs/scripting.md#L286)). |
| Change arena or play a sequence | `gd.kit` can draw text/panels over a match; the engine has `GS_CUTSCENE_*` states and camera calls used by Hand entry/death. `gd.scene_launch` jumps through a soft reset and can start a newly seeded VS/Training scene. Its 1P modes accept `mode=` only, so it cannot currently describe a customized Classic boss scene or resume Classic's run state ([kit](../docs/scripting.md#L193), [scene launch](../docs/scripting.md#L290), [scene grammar limit](scene-launch.md#L271), [Hand camera](../melee/src/melee/ft/ftbosslib.c#L429), [cutscene kinds](../melee/src/melee/gm/forward.h#L103)). | **S** for a kit overlay/interstitial in the same arena; **M** for a deterministic native camera/action timeline or explicit second-scene transition with reconstructed mode state; **L** for seamless mid-match stage replacement and arbitrary cinematic asset authoring. |
| Resume or finish | Native mode state exit decides the next major/minor scene. A custom mode can call the delayed match termination and route its exit to another fight, a custom result, or the existing clear mode. A `gd.scene_launch` jump instead resets to a new scene; `on_match_end` is called when the scene starts changing, too late to extend the old match ([Classic exit](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmclassic.c#L1029), [scene traversal](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_1A3F.c#L158), [Lua scene-end timing](../melee/pc/platform/gw_script.c#L4605)). | **M** to give a custom mode explicit phase result, retry and return routing. Reusing Classic's clear/save callback needs deliberate scoring policy. |

## 4. Risks to settle before the experiment grows

- **Camera and animation.** Hand entrance and death both take over the camera, freeze/unfreeze participants, switch game speed, hide HUD and add effects. A continuation must restore standard camera, speed, pause flags and input after each beat; any sleeping Master entity must not be targeted or shown. Crazy Hand's paired attacks expect Master Hand to be present by kind, so test behavior when Master is sleeping at zero HP ([controller](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L123), [camera reset](../melee/src/melee/ft/ftbosslib.c#L445), [paired AI](../melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCrazyHand/ftcrazyhandwait10.c#L201), [Sleep camera state](../melee/src/melee/ft/ftcolanim.c#L47)).
- **Music, background and HUD.** Boss FD external stage `0xB0` has special background HP thresholds and audio. `gm_8016ECE8` averages slots whose character IDs are Hands, including a zero-HP Hand that remains in its slot; a non-Hand follow-up would not itself drive those phases. The HP HUD depends on a special player flag and `Player_GetRemainingHP`, and the 1P controller explicitly toggles `hud_enabled`; a follow-up needs an intentional meter and music/cue policy ([FD threshold](../melee/src/melee/gr/grlast.c#L602), [HP average](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmvs.c#L2436), [audio](../melee/src/melee/lb/lbaudio_ax.c#L476), [HUD](../melee/src/melee/if/ifstatus.c#L564), [controller toggles](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L151)).
- **1P timer, score and records.** Classic's builder enables the encounter timer from matchup data, while the Hand finish explicitly disables it late. `gm_8017D7AC` folds the match's frame count and standing data into 1P stats; the final Classic callback can save a best time and clears/unlocks. Decide whether the new phase shares the same clock, awards points once, and affects existing Classic records. A custom mode should keep experimental records separate until that policy is fixed ([timer setup](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L598), [finish timer](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L212), [score/time accounting](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L893), [Classic record update](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmclassic.c#L1041)).
- **Netplay and rollback.** Current online rollback is scoped to one `GS_VS` match and closes when that scene ends. Lua memory is not snapshotted and script hooks do not run on resimulated frames; even `rollback_safe` gameplay writes are refused *inside queued event hooks*. A native phase flag, spawn decision, timers, RNG and any new HUD/game state must be deterministic and snapshot-covered; identical gameplay content must be agreed by peers. Keep the first experiment offline ([rollback scene gate](../melee/pc/platform/gw_rollback.c#L287), [Lua contract](../docs/scripting.md#L108), [event-write restriction](../melee/pc/platform/gw_script.c#L524), [Geno rollback state](../melee/docs/geno.md#L67)).

## 5. First experiment: Master Hand → Crazy Hand, same FD match

**Goal:** In a new, offline custom mode, defeat Master Hand and then fight **Crazy Hand as a second phase** on the same `0xB0` Final Destination arena. Do not alter ordinary Classic records or Classic's clear route. Crazy Hand is the best first `X` because Classic already preloads its assets, reserves slot 1, supplies a spawn initializer and has Hand-specific HP/HUD logic. It tests the scene-hold problem without first building a general fighter spawner ([Classic preload](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmclassic.c#L880), [slot arrangement](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L818), [Crazy initializer](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L68)).

1. Seed a custom mode's `GS_VS` fight with P1 and Master Hand on FD `0xB0`; preload Crazy Hand and leave slot 1 inactive. Set a local phase enum `MASTER`, `MASTER_DEATH`, `CRAZY_ENTRY`, `CRAZY`, `FINISH` in game/snapshot state. Use a single native controller, patterned on `fn_8017C1A4`, instead of layering an unrelated Lua timer over Classic's active controller.
2. In `MASTER`, when `Player_GetRemainingHP(2) <= 0`, latch the trigger **once** and enter `MASTER_DEATH`, bypassing vanilla state 7. Let Master Hand's existing death animation finish until its fighter is in invisible `Sleep`; retain the participant's input and regular camera. Disable the vanilla half-HP Crazy join in this mode only ([HP gate](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L159), [death/cleanup](../melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftMasterHand/ftmasterhanddamage0.c#L69), [Sleep](../melee/src/melee/ft/ftcolanim.c#L47)).
3. In `CRAZY_ENTRY`, initialize Crazy Hand in the reserved slot with a chosen HP (start with its existing difficulty attribute value), then let the existing entry motion play. Restore HUD, standard camera, game speed, input and timer policy before `CRAZY`. Watch for any attack that tries to coordinate with the sleeping Master and suppress or redirect that branch if needed ([existing spawn](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L68), [existing entry sequence](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L123), [paired AI](../melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCrazyHand/ftcrazyhandwait10.c#L201)).
4. In `CRAZY`, wait for `Player_GetRemainingHP(1) <= 0`, let Crazy's death animation/cleanup finish, then run one final camera/flash beat and call the normal match termination setters. The custom `on_exit` routes to a simple custom clear/return scene, recording one win only after the second defeat. Test early P1 KO, timeout, retry and leaving mid-cinematic as separate exit paths ([Crazy HP gate](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_17C0.c#L169), [Crazy cleanup](../melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCrazyHand/ftcrazyhandtaggrab.c#L62), [termination](../melee/src/melee/gm/gmvs.c#L286), [mode routing](../melee/src/melee/gm/gm_1A3F.c#L158)).

**Pass condition when eventually implemented/tested:** Master Hand reaches zero, completes its disappearance, Crazy Hand arrives without a scene reload, P1 retains control, Crazy's HP meter works, and the match ends once after Crazy reaches zero. Confirm camera/speed/music/HUD restoration and failure exits on a Windows game run; none was run for this research note.

## Ten-line summary

1. Master Hand and Crazy Hand are fighter kinds with dedicated motion states and boss AI.
2. Both use stamina-like HP: maximum HP minus accumulated damage, with special HUD flags.
3. Giga Bowser is a separate Bowser-derived fighter and an Adventure encounter, not a Hand-style HP boss.
4. Classic's fixed Hand arena is Final Destination external stage `0xB0`.
5. The Classic 1P controller owns entry, Crazy's conditional join, death beats and match termination.
6. Crazy joins below half the difficulty-selected Master HP only on eligible higher-difficulty, no-continue, fast runs.
7. Zero HP starts a Hand death motion; disappearance and the delayed final flash happen later.
8. A successful Classic clear switches to trophy fall, credits, ending movie and congratulations.
9. Lua/Geno can observe events and draw UI; in-scene hold, boss spawn and routing need native work.
10. First experiment: offline custom mode, Master dies, Crazy spawns in the reserved slot, then one final clear.
