# Sora (`trail`) jab chain: Ultimate data and the Marth-host gap

Research dated 2026-09-26. This note supersedes no earlier research note. It uses the user's extracted Ultimate data and the current, locally modified port tools as read-only sources. No repo code was edited and no game was run. Addresses in the ACMD section are Ghidra virtual addresses in the dumped script NRO; frame numbers there are **animation frames**, not elapsed real frames. “Confirmed” means directly present in the cited source. “Inferred” means the control path or gameplay interpretation still needs a status trace or live measurement.

Hash40 lookups for `game_attack100`, `game_attack100start`, `game_attack100end`, `game_attacks3s2`, and `game_attacks3s3` found no matching fighter-game entry in this dump. The S3 continuation names are **`game_attacks32` and `game_attacks33`**, not the `...s2/s3` spellings. The absence is scoped to these names and this dump, not to every possible alias.

`fighter_param_table[76]` gives `attack_combo_max=3`, `attack_combo_type=fighter_combo_type_succeed`, `s3_combo_max=3`, `combo_attack_12_end=38`, `combo_attack_13_end=36`, `combo_attack_32_end=44`, `combo_attack_33_end=40`, `attack100_type=False`, and `attack_100_enable_cnt=0` (XML lines 28251–28260 and 28568). The first pair of `combo_attack_*_end` values belong to the S1/jab stages by their labels; the `32/33` pair are for S3. `SUCCEED` is confirmed as the enum name/value class, not independently proven here to mean “on whiff” or to define a particular button test.

**Window interpretation.** Jab 1 raises the first combo flag at f20 and the no-hit flag at f22; its parameter end is f38. Jab 2 does the same at f16/f18 with end f36. These imply intended animation-frame windows of roughly **20–38 for jab 1→2 and 16–36 for jab 2→3** (no-hit permission beginning two frames later), subject to the common status's exact inclusive comparison and input buffering. Jab 1's f1–6 half-speed stretch adds roughly five elapsed frames to its later animation events. The f40/f38 motion-list cancel frames are later than the respective f38/f36 combo endpoints, so ordinary action cancel is a different route. No `ATTACK_COMBO` macro was emitted in these decompiled bodies; the observed operation is `WorkModule::on_flag` at `+0x720`/`+0x72c`. The current converter calls them combo and no-hit combo by inference (`ports/ir/tools/acmd_to_ftcmd.py:274-279`); resolving the constant table in this exact binary or the generic status would raise confidence.

**Movement.** The extracted `Trans.z` animation root reaches +3.5367 and returns to 0 by the end of jab 1 (50 sampled frames); jab 2 reaches +4.361 by its end (46 frames); jab 3 reaches +9.109 by its end (63 frames). Jab 2 and jab 3 therefore have forward travel authored in their distinct clips; jab 1 has a small temporary displacement and returns. Whether all of that root translation becomes world movement depends on motion flags and runtime handling. The data support GD's broad visual description, while “spinning backhand” still requires visual/pose analysis.

**Jab 2 to forward tilt.** There is **no explicit S1→S3 branch command** in `game_attack12` and no shared Attack13/S3 game script. The data show two independently configured three-stage chains. The stage-2 forward tilt clip is exactly the jab-2 clip, which can make the chains look related. A forward stick plus a new attack press after jab 2 becomes normally interruptible at its motion-list cancel frame 38 is the plausible ordinary forward-tilt input, **not a confirmed special jab-combo branch**. Whether a forward+A press during f16–36 chooses jab 3, S3, or is buffered for later remains unresolved without the generic `lua2cpp_common.nro` status/input-priority trace or a controlled Ultimate input test. Do not encode a jab2→ftilt branch from the present evidence.

## Melee and the present port

Melee common `Attack11` starts with `allow_interrupt=false`, `x2218_b1=false`, a countdown from `co_attrs.jab_2_input_window`, and a buffered-press flag (`melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_Attack1.c:113-128`). `checkAttack12` decrements the countdown, buffers a newly pressed A while positive, and enters Attack12 once both the buffer and `x2218_b1` are true (lines 211–225). Attack12 repeats that with `jab_3_input_window` and `checkAttack13` (lines 184–195, 236–250, 276–289). Those attributes are `ftCo_DatAttrs` fields at fighter offsets `+0x18c/+0x190` (`melee/src/melee/ft/types.h:791-792`), and Geno can override both (`melee/pc/geno/geno_game.c:585-586`). There is no general `attack_combo_max` attribute in this Melee path; the available third row and the state logic determine the endpoint.

Melee's `JabCombo` ftcmd is **opcode 29** (`29 << 26`, `0x74000000`; table dispatch subtracts `0xA` in `melee/src/melee/ft/ftaction.c:1349-1361`). Its handler `ftAction_80071AE8` sets `x2218_b1` when the command's `disabled` field is zero or `fp->x197C` is non-null (`ftaction.c:563-571`, `melee/src/melee/lb/types.h:682-685`). `cmd_vars[0..3]` are separate ftcmd variables, set by a different handler, `ftAction_80071820` (`ftaction.c:458-479`); the jab chain does **not** test a cmd var. The converter emits the JabCombo word at Sora's `+0x720` flags, f20/f16 (`ports/ir/tools/acmd_to_ftcmd.py:274-279`). Melee's rapid-jab check is separate and uses `rapid_jab_window` with `x2218_b2` (`melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_Attack100.c:160-171`); Ultimate Sora's `attack100_type=False` gives no reason to add a rapid jab.

Melee's `doAttack13` has a hard-coded **`Ft_Kind_Mars`** case that calls `doAttack12Rapid`, restarting Attack11; its default enters `ftCo_MS_Attack13` (`ftCo_Attack1.c:258-273`). The installer defaults to a distinct m-ex destination kind **52** (`ports/ir/tools/install_ultimate.py:382-390`), so the ordinary Sora installation should take the default Attack13 path even though it cloned Marth's data. A custom destination kind or another dispatch change would require checking `fp->kind` before treating the Mars exception as active. The root cause already visible is the empty host motion row: `_build/tmp/ir/marth.melee.ir.json` has Attack11 row 46 and Attack12 row 47 populated, but **Attack13 row 48 has `empty=true`, `clip=null`, flag `0x00000012` and no animation** (the IR's `behavior.subactions` entry; `ports/ir/tools/build_melee_fighter.py:219-249` defines this extraction). The report `_build/tmp/sora-framedata-check.md:61` says `Attack13 | game_attack13 | export_missing | 46 / 1`; it did not observe a third jab. That report is evidence of absence in that LAB export, not a fresh reproduction or a proof of every input path.

The converter **does produce** Attack13: `ROWS` maps row 48 to `game_attack13` (`ports/ir/tools/acmd_to_ftcmd.py:324-326`), and `_build/tmp/ir/trail.moveset.json:117-132` contains row 48 with 11 ftcmd words and clip `c00attack13`. The installer, however, seeds its animation `rows` only when the host row already has a relocated, nonempty animation pointer and nonzero size (`ports/ir/tools/install_ultimate.py:465-475`). It then iterates that set to choose clips (`:485-507`, `:538-575`); row 48 is excluded. It later writes **the script pointer** for every moveset row, including 48 (`:677-685`). Thus the current pipeline can leave row 48 with Sora's Attack13 script but no animation/motion record. Motion-list `move=true` for all three jab clips also needs its normal animation-driven flag treatment when row 48 is filled (`install_ultimate.py:515-575`).

Melee's `Attack12_IASA` checks forward tilt before `checkAttack13` once `allow_interrupt` is true (`ftCo_Attack1.c:236-250`). Its forward-tilt input is a newly pressed A with forward stick above `p_ftCommonData->x98`, within the tilt angle range (`melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_AttackS3.c:26-58`). This gives a concrete **Melee-side** route by which a late forward+A after jab 2 appears as forward tilt rather than jab 3. It is not evidence of an Ultimate-specific branch.

## Smallest general design to test

1. **Fill an explicitly targeted empty common row.** Let `install_ultimate.py` include a host motion row when a translated moveset explicitly supplies both its clip and script, even if the host row was empty. Populate row 48's animation pointer, offset, size, script pointer and animation-driven movement flag through the existing row pipeline. Validate that only rows named in the moveset are admitted and the clip exists. This generalizes to any imported fighter whose host lacks a source row; it does not invent a fighter-specific Attack13 state. `trail.moveset.json` already supplies the content.
2. **Use common Attack13 if Sora's runtime kind takes the default path.** For an m-ex kind distinct from `Ft_Kind_Mars`, the existing `checkAttack13` and `Fighter_ChangeMotionState(ftCo_MS_Attack13)` path is the smallest behavior change: no Geno state is necessary. If this port's runtime kind does hit the Mars case, add a **profile-gated** PC override to `doAttack13` that chooses the common Attack13 only when the port declares a populated third jab; keep vanilla Marth's two-jab loop. Avoid making every Marth-host clone acquire jab 3.
3. **Match the input window only after confirming the row.** Keep the converter's f20/f16 JabCombo event and configure Geno `jab_2_input_window` / `jab_3_input_window` so an A press remains bufferable through the Ultimate f38/f36 endpoint. Melee's counter counts IASA calls from state entry, while jab 1 runs at half animation speed f1–6 and hitlag can change elapsed time, so copying `38`/`36` directly into the counters is **not yet justified**. First verify static events and runtime kind, then measure the actual buffer and transition frames in a later authorized game run.
4. **Do not add a jab-to-S3 branch yet.** The confirmed S3 follow-ups are separate scripts and S3 has its own max of three. Preserve ordinary forward+A after cancel; inspect `lua2cpp_common.nro` input priority if exact Ultimate behavior during jab 2's f16–36 window is required. A full Geno jab state becomes appropriate only if that trace shows branch/precedence or frame behavior the common Melee state cannot express.

No code or game validation was performed for this note. The proposed first static checks are: row 48 has a real animation pointer and `c00attack13`; its ftcmd begins at f10 and clears at f18; the third-jab transition uses the intended runtime kind path; and the profile windows outlive their f20/f16 JabCombo gates. A later on-screen/controller check is needed before calling the chain fixed.

## Five-line summary

Ultimate Sora has a distinct three-hit jab: `game_attack11` → `game_attack12` → `game_attack13`.
Its jab continuation flags open at animation f20 and f16; fighter-param endpoints are f38 and f36.
Forward tilt is a separate three-hit S3 chain whose second stage reuses jab 2's animation.
Marth's Attack13 motion row is empty; the converter writes its script, but the installer skips its clip.
Populate that row and use the common state with a profile-gated Mars exception only if runtime kind requires it.

## Follow-up: installed kind and three-stage forward tilt (2026-09-26)

**Installed kind, high confidence.** The installed Sora mod at `_build/agents/alpha/sora/mods-marth/ultimate-trail-slot/INSTALL.json:5-7` records `row.internal=52`, `row.external=51`; its `slot_files.row` repeats 52/51 at lines 8-12, and its `mod.json:10` describes the same m-ex row with Marth as the host. The installer defaults `--dst-k` to 52 and passes it to `mk_slot_files.py` (`ports/ir/tools/install_ultimate.py:404,448`); the clone tool writes the destination kind into MxDt (`ports/halberd/tools/mk_slot_files.py:39,67-74`). The current Sora scene uses `p1=mex:52` (`docs/prompts/codex-sora-performance.md:33-34`). Marth's source kind is 18 (`install_ultimate.py:66-67`, `melee/src/melee/ft/forward.h:121`, `/* 12 */` hexadecimal). `doAttack13` switches on **`fp->kind`**, sends only `Ft_Kind_Mars` to the two-jab loop, and sends all other kinds to common Attack13 (`melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_Attack1.c:258-273`). The installed kind 52 therefore takes the common Attack13 case; cloning Marth's data does not by itself invoke the kind-18 case. This is a static control-path conclusion, not a game-run observation.

**Empty-row implementation scope, high confidence from the offline inputs.** The full `_build/tmp/ir/trail.moveset.json` names nine empty Marth IR rows with a real script and clip: 48 (`c00attack13`), 53/54/56/57 (`c01attacks3s`), and 60/61/63/64 (`c03attacks4s`). The row admission in `ports/ir/tools/install_ultimate.py` now follows exactly those explicit entries; a clip-only `--row-clips` entry has no translated script and cannot cause an empty row to be admitted. The jab-only subset of rows 46-48 admits only 48. The installed binary/mod was not rebuilt or run in this follow-up.

**Forward tilt data, high confidence for values, medium for flag meaning.** The ACMD index has `game_attacks3`, `game_attacks32`, and `game_attacks33` at `${GW_GHIDRA_PROJECTS}/sora_acmd/index.tsv:18-20`. The fighter-param table for `fighter_kind_trail` has `s3_combo_max=3`, `combo_attack_32_end=44`, and `combo_attack_33_end=40` (`_build/tmp/trail-jab-fighter-param.xml:28252,28256-28257`). In the stage-1 decomp (`${GW_GHIDRA_PROJECTS}/sora_acmd/game/0x5b268858f__0xd28325277.c:118-126,752-770`), the `+0x720` flag is raised at animation f29 only in the branch selected by the `+0x710` work flag; the other branch has no observed `+0x720` there. Stage 2 raises the same flag at f18 (`.../game/0x5b268858f__0xed49c68c0.c:708-724`); stage 3 has no follow-up flag. The `+0x720` name **ENABLE_COMBO** is inferred from the command context and constant registry, as in the earlier section; the exact `+0x710` meaning is unresolved. Melee's `ftCo_AttackS3_CheckInput` enters a single AttackS3 on a fresh A press with forward stick (`melee/src/melee/ft/kinds/ftCommon/ftCo_AttackS3.c:26-58`), and its IASA only returns to normal Wait inputs when `allow_interrupt` is set (lines 95-100). It has no second or third S3 state.

**Proposed chain, medium confidence pending Ultimate common-status input trace.** Keep Melee's forward-plus-new-A entry into the ordinary `AttackS3S` row for stage 1. For a Sora-only profile, translate stage 1's selected ACMD branch into a persistent Geno `CHG PRESSED ATTACK` check beginning when the observed `+0x720` gate appears at animation f29, targeting a Geno stage-2 state; clear that check after the `combo_attack_32_end` boundary f44. Stage 2 uses `game_attacks32` hitboxes and the shared `c00attack12` clip, then registers the same **new A press** check at f18 through its f40 `combo_attack_33_end` boundary to enter stage 3. Stage 3 uses `game_attacks33` and `c01attacks33`, and registers no continuation. The Geno profile already supports per-state subactions and scripts (`ports/ir/tools/trail_specials_geno.py:198-208,323-330`), button-press condition 4 and ATTACK mask bit 0, and persistent `CHG` plus `CHGCLR` (`melee/docs/geno.md:1228-1236,1253-1260`). Marth host rows 314/315 are candidate stage-2/3 subactions because the current Sora specials map does not allocate them (`trail_specials_geno.py:198-208`; `_build/tmp/ir/marth.melee.ir.json` rows 314-315); they need explicit clip, script, ground physics, and the stage-2 root travel treatment when allocated. Keep the stage-1 `+0x710` condition: the branch without `+0x720` should not gain a continuation by accident. A fresh A press is the proposed advance input, not yet proved to be Ultimate's exact buffering or stick-priority rule; the exact inclusive/exclusive f44/f40 cutoff and any early-press buffer require `lua2cpp_common.nro` status analysis before claiming frame-perfect parity. Do not infer a jab-2-to-tilt branch merely because ftilt stage 2 reuses the jab-2 clip.
