The fight HUD

Step 5 of the UI design. Two choices: where things sit (H1 or H2) and how Heat is drawn (M1 or M2). The map, the shop, results and pause are not designed yet.

White with an ink edge: ours. Coral is P1, lagoon is P2. Ink with a white edge: a foe, or the keystone wedge. Sunny: something is ready or just fired. Heat: Cold, Warm, Hot, Blazing.

Each player owns a bottom corner. What the party shares (stocks, keystone, coins) sits between them. Foes line up along the top, where a boss bar also goes.

One rule to learn: bottom is us, top is them. It holds from a solo duel to four foes to a boss without anything moving.

On the fighter

The moments: a drive glowing when it is ready, the fire, the chain line running drive to drive, the technique spark, and a flame that grows with the Heat state. Eyes are already there.

In the HUD row

The facts the orbit cannot hold still: slot order left to right, level pips, and exact charge as a ring. A badge only flashes sunny on the frame it fires.

No numbers but percent

Heat has no digits, as the design note rules: four flame sizes and three marks say it. The only other number is coins, and it changes only at a cash-out.

The Heat meter

One per player, next to their percent. Heat rises 1 for each 1% dealt and falls 1 for each 1% taken; it never drains on its own. Both candidates use the same colours, the same three marks and the same flame.

Chosen

H2, our corners and their top. It is the one layout that does not change shape between a duel, a pack and a boss, and it keeps each player's percent, Heat and drives in one corner they can find without reading.

M1, the bar. It leaves the percent as the biggest thing on screen and shows gain, loss and a raised cap the plainest way there is: longer or shorter.

Original vector art. Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque, Source Sans 3 (SIL OFL). The stage and fighters are placeholders with invented names; drive loadouts, percents and coin counts are made up for the mock. Heat thresholds (25, 60, 110, cap 150) and the one-for-one loss follow the design note and the owner's ruling.
