Step 10: the tag

The swap works, and it feels apart from the fight. Today's tag, then four things it could become. Each row shows the input, three frames of play, the HUD part and the rule in one line.

Today

D-pad down, when standing or falling. The game stops for up to a second.
Gives
Nothing, unless you wear Slipstream or Relay.
Limit
5 seconds between tags.
Why it drags
The steering thumb does it, it cannot cancel a move, and it is a cutscene.

The input

The left thumb leaves the stick, presses down, presses down again to skip, then travels back.

What happensA and B are your pair, the dark one is the foe

A lands a hit and has to finish the whole move first.

Everything stops behind bars and a name card, for you and for player 2.

B stands still. Your thumb is on the D-pad, not the stick.

On the HUD

A small sticker beside the Heat bar. It says who is benched and nothing else.

1Quick Tag

Tag when you are free to act, or right after you deal damage.
Gives
Nothing new. This fixes the hands only.
Limit
3 seconds between tags.
Build
Small. One button masked, one check loosened, no freeze.
Risk
Easy to do, still no reason to.

The input

Y is Tag; X and the stick still jump. The right thumb tags, the left never lets go. A press is kept for 8 frames.

What happensA and B are your pair, the dark one is the foe

A lands a hit. Press Y during it or just after.

B is there on the next frame. No pause, no card. Speed and facing carry over.

B follows up at once, still drifting the way you were holding.

On the HUD

The same sticker, with the Tag button on it. It turns sunny while a tag would cancel your move.

2Assist Strike

Tag: your partner jumps in beside you and strikes once. Costs 30 Heat.
Gives
A second body. Its damage fills your Heat and charges its own drive row.
Limit
30 Heat a call. No cooldown.
Build
Large. Two live fighters on one port, one of them scripted.
Risk
The engine work, then a smash on demand.

The input

Tap Y: B strikes and leaves. Hold Y: B stays, A leaves. The stick picks the strike: side, up or down.

What happensA and B are your pair, the dark one is the foe

A lands a hit with Heat to spend.

B appears behind A, already swinging. You keep moving A the whole time.

B's strike fires B's drives, then B is gone. A is already on the foe.

On the HUD

A mark at 30 on the Heat bar. The bench sticker shows B's drives charging.

3Backline

Your partner backs you up from the bench. Swap when you come back from a lost stock.
Gives
Two drive rows firing. The bench's row charges at half speed.
Limit
Half charge. No swap in the middle of a stock.
Build
Small to medium. Mostly rules; the swap moves to the respawn.
Risk
More power, fewer decisions. No two-fighter combos.

The input

Nothing to press in a fight. D-pad down swaps only on the respawn platform, where nothing is chasing you.

What happensA and B are your pair, the dark one is the foe

A fights. B's drives ride a wide, dim ring around A.

A bench drive fills and fires by itself at the foe A just hit.

A stock is lost. On the platform, press down to send B in at 0%.

On the HUD

The bench sticker becomes B's drive row.

4Drop it

One fighter per player in a fight. Choose who fights at each stop.
Gives
A free D-pad direction, one row to read, and the three paired fighters become playable.
Loses
Two-fighter combos, hiding a hurt fighter, two drives, a keystone and a Unique.
Build
Deletion, plus rewriting the rules that mention a tag.
Risk
Solo runs get thinner.

The input

The tag button is gone. D-pad down can hold the second active drive.

What happensA and B are your pair, the dark one is the foe

One fighter, one row. A fight plays like a fight.

The fight ends. B has not been on screen.

At the stop, pick who fights next. Both keep their own drives.

On the HUD

No bench sticker in a fight. It moves to the road screen.

Test Quick Tag first, then add Handoff

The hands are cheap to fix and the answer decides everything else. If the quick tag feels right, one sentence joins it to the build:

Handoff: tag right after you deal damage and the newcomer’s first drive fires.

That is the one-body half of Assist Strike, on the cooldown that already exists. Relay becomes the big version of it.

The ten-minute test

Solo, fight 1, a pair you know, two cheap drives each.

Tag buttonY
Freeze2 frames, no card
A press is kept for8 frames
Tag out of a move after damage20 frames
Between tags3 seconds

Run and tag. Launcher, Y, aerial. Throw, Y, follow-up. Then the same with Handoff on.

What the answer leads to

  1. Easy to do, and I tag for what it fires: build Handoff. Assist Strike can wait.
  2. Easy to do, still beside the point: Backline. Both fighters and both builds stay.
  3. Backline falls flat too: drop it, as a roster of two with one per fight.
