Recommended

N2, the next-up poster. With a straight path there is nothing to choose on the map, so its job is to say what the next fight is, in words, and get out of the way. The poster does that and stays put in every state.

S1, one counter. It is the camp list again: one cursor, full drive text, any fighter. It also makes the shared purse safe, which S2 cannot do without splitting the coins per player.

Original vector art. Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque, Source Sans 3 (SIL OFL). Fighters, foes, bosses and stages are placeholders with invented names; prices, coin counts, times and result numbers are made up for the mock. Assumed here: the rest stop sits just before the boss, a drive row holds four, the party holds one keystone per act reached, and coins are one shared purse.
