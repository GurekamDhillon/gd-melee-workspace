Step 8: where the run goes

The fights are good. This sheet is the layer above them: three ways to give a run a road, different rewards, and a reason to start the next one. Screens are 640 by 480 game pixels at actual size.

What a choice is for

Today every reward is a drive pick. These eight are what a road can pay. Two are scarce on purpose, level-ups and stocks, so a road is about power, safety or money.

Drive pickOne of three. Every fight won banks one. The floor of every road.
Family pickOne of three from one family. How a road or a region feeds a build.
Rare pickOne of three, Rare or better. Only from danger: steep roads, dares, elites.
Level-upRaise a drive you wear. The scarce one: late growth is levels.
CoinsCommon. The shop turns them into anything, at a poor rate.
StockThe run's life. Scarce: one elite a loop, the shop, a loop cleared.
Drive slotBosses 1 and 2 only. Three slots become five.
KeystoneBosses only. Later bosses raise a keystone you hold.

M1. The forked road

At each stop the road splits into two or three posters. Pick one: you see its fights and what it banks.

One loopa coral A is a decision

You decide

Which of three roads to the next stop, and which of two elites. Twice a loop, a few seconds each, at the stop you were already at.

New screens

None. The road screen shows three narrow posters in place of one.

It needs

Fight kinds that differ (duel, pack, shift room, waves, elite) and four reward kinds in the bank. Mostly planned already.

Two players

Player 1 holds the cursor. Player 2 plants a flag on the road they want.

Endless loops

From loop 2 a third road, from loop 4 roads with two twists and two prizes.

Size and risk

Small. The risk is roads that differ only in numbers, so one is always right.

M2. Regions

Each loop is one themed region picked from a small map. The region changes the stages, the foes, the drops and the boss.

One loopa coral A is a decision

You decide

The region for the whole loop, from three on offer. Once a loop, after the boss.

New screens

The region map. A region name on the banner and the loop strip. A page in the collection.

It needs

Five or six regions, each with stages, a wave mix, a rule, a drop tilt, an elite and a boss. Today there is one real boss.

Two players

Player 1 holds the cursor. Player 2 plants a flag.

Endless loops

A region met again is its deeper form: its rule doubled, its elite with one more drive.

Size and risk

Large, and most of it is content. The risk is five regions that are one boss in five coats.

M3. Dares

The road stays one line. Before each session you may sign a dare: a harder rule for those fights, and a prize at the stop.

One loopa coral A is a decision

You decide

Whether to sign a dare for the next session, and which. Twice a loop.

New screens

The dare list, as the last step of the stop. A small ink tag in the fight HUD while a dare is on.

It needs

Ten to twelve dares. Most are switches the rules already have: foe size and damage, one more foe, no tag, a starting percent.

Two players

Both must hold A to sign. One player can never dare for the other.

Endless loops

Dares come in three weights. Goals open the heavy ones and the double dare.

Size and risk

Small to medium. The risk is that it reads as a punishment menu, and that always or never signing is right.

Build M1, with dares as its twists

The forked road is the plain one: no new screen, a choice read in one look, taken at a stop you were already at. A steep road is a dare: its twist and its prize are printed on the poster, so M3 needs no list of its own.

Regions come last, as the coat a loop wears, once wave fights and a second boss exist.

The smallest slice to play

At the stop before fights 1 to 3, two posters. The open road: duel, pack, duel, three picks. The steep road: the same three fights, every foe carries one more drive; it banks two picks and a level-up.

It uses only fights and rewards that exist. The new parts are a second poster, left and right, and a level-up token in the bank.

Then, in this order

  1. The elite fork: two elites, each showing the drive and the prize it banks.
  2. Goals and bests at the camp.
  3. Wave fights as the swarm road.
  4. Kits.
  5. Regions.
