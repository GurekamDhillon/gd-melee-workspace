Envoy on the main menu

Step 2 of the UI design. Two choices: the row label (L1, L2 or L3) and the panel layout (P1 or P2). The camp, cards and HUD are not designed yet.

Grey is retail and comes from the player's disc: plates, highlight, motion, bars. Not ours to restyle. Colour is drawn by the port: the label image, the panel, the description text.

The row label

Envoy is the third of six rows. Each candidate is shown large in its three states, then at game size in the column: once with the cursor on Vs. Mode, once on Envoy.

Widescreen. Nothing changes size. The panel keeps its 4:3 position and scale next to the plate column and is never stretched; the extra width is retail background. If the retail menu is found to stretch at 16:9 instead, the port's label, panel and text should still be drawn unstretched, anchored to the plate column's right edge.

Recommended

Label L3, wordmark and emblem. It lets the retail plate stay a retail plate while one small sticker says this row is different, and that sticker doubles as the charge ring on hover and the disc mark when the disc is missing.

Panel P1, box lid. The retail panel leaves a hole when it is hidden, and one solid shape fills it; its fixed top-to-bottom order holds all four states and stays readable at 640 by 480.

Original vector art. Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque, Source Sans 3 (SIL OFL). Grey plates, bars and backgrounds are neutral placeholders for retail parts; fighters are neutral silhouettes; sibling row names are set in the sheet's own type.
