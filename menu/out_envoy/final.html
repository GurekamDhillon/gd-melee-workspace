Envoy, the whole UI

The final design: every choice from steps 2 to 6 in one flow, with the steps' disagreements settled. Each screen is 640 by 480 game pixels, shown here at 1.1 times.

White with an ink edge: ours. Ink with a white edge: a foe, or a keystone. Sunny: the cursor, or something ready. Coral: Player 1. Lagoon: Player 2. Grey: retail, a placeholder.

The flow, in play order

Ten screens. The line between two says which button takes you on. Between fights there is no character select: fight, pick of three, the keystone pick after a boss, map, fight.
