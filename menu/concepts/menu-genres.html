Five menus that are not the hub

The current top level is a hub: one hero tile plus a column that steps down ×0.76. That is one answer out of several. Below are five structurally different answers — different navigation models, not reskins — each drawn as a live 640×480 stage. These are prototypes, not pictures. The layout rules are executable, the motion runs at the frame counts in the spec, and the item-count slider stress-tests each rule from 2 to 9. Drive them with the arrow keys and A / B.

A · Orbit

ROTATIONAL

The cursor travels a ring instead of a grid. Position on the ring is the menu index, so wrap-around is visible rather than implied, and the selected card swings forward to a fixed reading slot. Cards past the front lose their labels — the ring is a transport, the front card is the page.

LAYOUT RULE

  • Ring centre (320, 300), radius 205, arc ±78°
  • Front slot (262, 120) 116×150 — every selection lands on exactly this rect
  • Label by rank: 0 = hero 44, 1 = heading 32, 2 = label 20, ≥3 = icon only
  • Alpha hard-stepped 1 → .70 → .42 → .30 → .22 over ranks 0–4

NAVIGATION + MOTION

  • ◄ ► / stick X rotate one step, 8f, the card travelling its own arc
  • Selected: TRA to front slot + SCA 1→1.30 overshoot at f4, settle f9 — the numbers row_select already ships
  • Wrap is a 18f full turn, so it reads as a rotation not a jump
  • A dives in, B backs out

FEASIBILITY

  • Direct. Every card is a parallelogram, so fp_draw_quad maps it exactly
  • Direct. TRA / SCA / ALPHA already exist as joint channels
  • Blocked: per-card rotation. The fan of upright cards is what you see here; it works, but a real orbit wants FJC_ROT

VERDICT

The strongest first impression of the five, and the only one that makes the top level feel like a fighting game. Wrap becomes legible. Costs: ~8 max; at n=9 you get 6 names and 3 blank cards; and it breaks the pad-script convention fe_menu_sweep.py assumes.

B · Filmstrip

1-D TRANSPORT

A media-browser shape. One large "now showing" card on the left, the upcoming items receding to the right in scale and alpha. The cursor is lateral only — the strip slides and the chosen card flies into the reading slot.

LAYOUT RULE

  • Reading slot (58, 88) 308×308; strip origin x = 404, pitch 70
  • Strip card 70×96, decaying ×0.86 per step; only 3 fit before the right edge
  • Alpha .60 → .40 → .24, hard-stepped
  • Selected gets an 8px gold left edge — never colour alone

NAVIGATION + MOTION

  • ◄ ► scrolls the strip one slot over 7 frames — kit_motion's existing scroll
  • Leaving a card: TRA to the reading slot 10f, SCA .6→1.0, alpha crossfade
  • Strip shift and card flight share one clock — no queued state

FEASIBILITY

  • Cheapest of the five. The list template with a scale channel — fk_frame's highlight glide is already this motion
  • Blocked: strictly 1-D. Anything hierarchical needs a second screen

VERDICT

The most modern and least risky, but the least identity — it reads as a generic launcher and drops the "place" idea entirely (no breadcrumb, because there is no hierarchy). Best kept for a leaf screen like Records or stage select, not the front door.

C · Index

EDITORIAL / ARCHIVE

No faces, no icons, no tiles. A contents page: a rule down the left margin, a section numeral, item rows set as pure typography with index numbers right-aligned in the mono face. The cursor is a gold square in the margin. It is today's list template with the chrome taken away.

LAYOUT RULE

  • Margin rule x = 88, 2px, from y 96 to the last row
  • Row pitch 48; hairline above each row, 2px gold under the selected
  • Label at x = 104 (label 20); selected row shifts +10 and goes gold
  • Index numeral right-aligned at x = 540 in Hasklug row
  • Description as a pull-quote: 4px gold rule + body, 2 lines / 88 chars

NAVIGATION + MOTION

  • ▲ ▼ only — no second axis, so the simplest model of the five
  • Cursor move is row_select unchanged: 10f, face gold_lt at f1 → gold at f8
  • Label TRA_X +10 over 6f — the label moves, the hairline does not

FEASIBILITY

  • Almost free. list_layout.json with the face/plate/icon quads deleted. The engine already builds list rows in fm_build
  • Zero new textures — body + row + label are already resident on every screen
  • Best accessibility — the margin tick and the indent both encode selection; no colour-only cue anywhere

VERDICT

The only one strictly cheaper than what ships, and the only one that scales to 12 rows without inventing rules — the slider goes past 9 to prove it. Its weakness is identity: pure typography with no pictograms reads as unfinished beside 29 ico_* bodies that already exist.

D · Dashboard

HARDWARE METAPHOR

The controller is the menu. Its buttons are the items, the D-pad is the cursor, the shoulders page. Direct positional targeting — you point at the button you want and it lights. Nothing about it is a list or a grid; it is the object on your desk.

LAYOUT RULE

  • Pad body (96, 78)–(500, 268), drawn from a 512×512 I4 mask (128 KB)
  • Items bind to named anchors: btn_a/b/x/y, dpad_*, shoulder_l/r, stick, home
  • Each anchor is centre + radius 22; hit-test is centre distance < 22
  • Header slab width follows the lit button's label — fm_chrome_panel already sizes it from the title

NAVIGATION + MOTION

  • Direct. No d-pad steps — pointing at a button is selecting it
  • Stick and D-pad both resolve to the nearest anchor within 28px
  • Lit button: SCA 1→1.15 about its centre over 6f, colour → gold — the toggle_set knob motion, reused verbatim
  • Shoulders page between hubs with a 7f cross-slide

FEASIBILITY

  • Draws today. One new I4 mask + a flat rect per anchor; the player needs no change
  • Direct. fms_in_verts already hit-tests quads for the mouse, so the pointer path is the same code
  • Blocked: the mask must be I4, not RGBA8 — at 2× a 512×512 RGBA8 pad is 1 MB and blows the entire per-screen budget on its own

VERDICT

The most original, and the best fit for how anyone actually holds a pad. Colour-blind safe for free, because buttons are identified by shape and position and the name is always in the header. Costs: cannot hold a list of 10, unusable by keyboard without a second path, every item needs a bespoke anchor. Too precious for the front door — but genuinely strong for a lobby or pause menu, where the items really are buttons.

E · Deck

DIMENSIONAL

A hand of cards fanned from the bottom of the screen. Depth order is cursor order, so the menu reads as a physical stack. The selection pulls a card clear of the fan and brings it forward.

LIVE CHECK

LAYOUT RULE

  • Fan origin (268, 214), pitch −52 x, −8 y going back
  • Card 96×130; selected 150×200 at (86, 132)
  • Fan depth by rank: #4a2c05 → #683d04, four hard steps, no gradient
  • A caption "n MORE" counts what does not fit

NAVIGATION + MOTION

  • ◄ ► walks the fan one card, and the whole fan moves — the motion is on the fan, not on a cursor
  • Selected: TRA out + SCA 1→1.34, 1.3× overshoot at f4, settle f9
  • The card that was selected re-enters the fan from the front, 8f behind
  • Detail pane: alpha 0→1 over 6f, starting 4f after the card

FEASIBILITY

  • Direct if the fan is TRA + SCA only — a spread without rotation, which is what is drawn here
  • Blocked: the version that reads as a real hand of cards needs per-card rotation. The player has FJC_TRA/SCA/ALPHA/RECOIL and nothing else
  • Also blocked: rotated textured quads break the "every textured quad is a parallelogram" rule the whole pipeline checks — this would be the first art needing non-affine UV mapping

VERDICT

The best fit to the subject — a fighting game's menu as a hand of match cards is immediately legible, and depth-as-cursor teaches itself in one move. Costs: occlusion by design, a hard cap near 7, and the only genuine engine work of the five. Prototype the un-rotated spread first — it is free, and if the idea survives without rotation the whole question closes.

What all five leave unchanged: the list template, the settings row widgets, the dialog, the toast, the breadcrumb and the footer hints. Rules has more rows than any of these hubs has tiles, and those screens are untouched by any of the five choices. Press A in any concept to see the child screen it has to hand off to.
