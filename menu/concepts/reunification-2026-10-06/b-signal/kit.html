Motion notes: what moves, how fast. Reduced motion turns every move into a 0 ms cut and keeps the focus bar
Focus moves 70 msThe bar jumps and the type steps between sizes (20 to 56) with an ease-out. Nothing waits for it.
Screen change 110 msThe new content slides 24 px in from the side you moved toward; the old one is a cut. Left and right are always the same direction.
Dialog 160 msThe scrim fades to 90% and the question slides 16 px up. Back dismisses in 80 ms.
Corner note 180 ms inSlides in from the edge, holds 2.4 s, leaves in 120 ms. At most one note at a time; a second replaces the first.
Press 40 msA 2 to 3 px shift and an inverse fill. No colour change, no bounce.
Hub peek 110 msMoving the focus in a hub swaps the peek column with the same slide.
