> **Release candidate 4.** This is a test build of 0.3.0, not the final release. Expect rough edges; the final notes follow below and may change before 0.3.0 ships.

> **Online play changed in 0.3.** Netplay protocol is now 6. **0.2.x players cannot play online with 0.3**, and 0.3 players cannot play 0.2.x: update both sides. Windows and Linux 0.3 play each other.

> **Public test build.** Please report bugs with what you did, which disc you used, and the crash report from `crashlogs` (or `melee-pc.log` from the game folder).

### New

- **UCF 0.84 is on by default offline.** The ruleset most players expect from tournament Melee (the shield-drop and dash-back fixes) now applies in offline matches. Turn it off in Settings, or with the toggle in the launcher. Online matches use the host's room rules, as before.
- **Six fighters in one match.** Matches with up to six fighters, including custom ones, now fit in memory.
- **Mod browser (SSBM Nucleus), much bigger.** Built with permission from sc00p; mods come from https://ssbmnucleus.net.
  - **Stage skins, effects, menus and audio** install from the in-game browser, with category tabs. A post the game cannot install tells you why.
  - **Custom stages** install as mods and work on your own vanilla disc.
  - **Mod fighters** (opt-in, off until you turn them on) install as mods on your own vanilla disc. Fighters that share the original skeleton are supported; others may be refused with a reason. There is a cap of 31 mods at once.
  - **Posts that are only a link** to an outside site cannot be installed, and the browser says so.
- **Mod sync for online rooms.** When you connect directly, the room shows a MODS DIFFER panel if your mods and the host's do not match. The game can install the host's mods for you, by their Nucleus post id only (nothing is sent between players), then restart and rejoin. Your own mod list is restored afterwards.
- **`.gdlp` replays (format 2).** Record a whole match, or a LAB session, and play it back. Replays carry Slippi-style events and can be exported to `.slp` from the LAB. Rewind keeps all four controllers' inputs.
- **Template fighters.** Fox, Falco, Marth, Jigglypuff and the Zelda/Sheik pair now exist as template fighters you can copy to make your own, each checked frame by frame against the original game's behaviour.
- **LAB.** Records inputs (`.gdlp`), steps CLIPS frame by frame with a mirrored pad, and Play in LAB / VS CPU can be started from the Workbench.
- **Engine effects.** Bloom tint and radius, ribbon trails with taper, soft particles, dissolve, rings, screen shake and flash, and room for 96 emitters at once.

### Workbench and launcher

- The Workbench (Geno fighter editor) has a new look, a working Play button at real speed, hitbox spheres by priority with launch arrows and meteors, hitbox element, shield damage and ground/air fields, combo routes and a Combo tab, a crash restore, and Spanish strings.
- The Workbench starts the newest engine it finds and recovers if the engine dies.
- Projectiles are fighter parts in Blender, and the Blender add-on has a Geno workspace.
- The launcher has a toggle for UCF and starts the game as before. Spanish labels are kept in step.

### Fixed

- **A second match no longer crashes.** Starting a second match in a session (even Mario against Luigi) could crash when a loaded archive was moved in memory.
- **Black band in Final Destination** on windows of odd sizes.
- **Rendering no longer changes offline game logic.** Visual randomness has its own stream.
- **Daisy and Knuckles crashes** and a hitbox slot clash on a custom fighter are fixed. Daisy and Skull Kid can float.
- Fighters whose throws use stand-in animations load them on demand, which is what lets six of them fit in one match.

### Known issues

- Mods installed from the browser take effect after a restart.
- Fighters from Nucleus that use a different skeleton from the original are limited; online play refuses mod fighters.
- Some Falcon animations cannot be previewed in the Workbench yet and are marked as such.
- Everything listed under "Known issues" in 0.2.0, 0.2.1 and 0.2.2 still applies, except fullscreen.
