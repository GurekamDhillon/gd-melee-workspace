### Fixed in 0.1.3
- **The GameCube adapter now reliably follows the window you're using.** In 0.1.2, switching windows (for example to Dolphin, or between two copies of the game) sometimes left the adapter stuck in the window you'd left. Now the window in front gets the controller within about a quarter of a second, every time.
- **The adapter is only kept in the background during an online match.** Searching for an opponent, the lobby and the menus now let it go when you alt-tab, like everywhere else.

Everything from 0.1.2 applies: see its notes for the other changes and known issues.
