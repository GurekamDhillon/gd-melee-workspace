> **Public test build.** Please report bugs: what you did, which disc, and the crash report from the `crashlogs` folder (or `melee-pc.log` from the game folder).

### New in 0.1.4
- **Online lobby: starters and counterpicks.** The stage list is split into STARTERS (Battlefield, Final Destination, Dream Land, Yoshi's Story, Fountain of Dreams) and COUNTERPICK (Pokémon Stadium). Game 1 strikes over the starters only, 1-2-1; the counterpick opens from game 2 for bans and picks.
- **The strike cursor skips stages you can't pick.** Struck and banned stages (and the counterpick in game 1) are jumped over, in every direction, with wrap-around.
- **Sheik can be picked online.** On the character select, press A on Zelda again to switch to Sheik (holding A while pressing START also works). The old "hold A while the match loads" swap is off online, where it could desync.
- **The launcher speaks Spanish.** Language follows Windows by default; English or Spanish can be chosen in the launcher. The game itself stays in English.

### Fixed in 0.1.4
- **Kirby's ground hammer is visible again.** It was drawn before its graphics were ready and was gone by the time they were. Held items now wait for their graphics instead.

Everything from 0.1.3 applies: see its notes for the other changes and known issues.
